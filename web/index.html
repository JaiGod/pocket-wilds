What's in the Wilds

24 original creatures
12 evolution lines, each drawn in code from shapes: no sprites, no borrowed designs.
Bonding Charms
Weaken a wild creature, then toss a Bonding, Kinship or Soulbond Charm. Low HP means better odds.
A whole region
Two towns, four routes, the winding Hollowdeep Cave and the summit of Crown Spire.
3 Wardens + a Champion
Earn the Grove, Deep and Sky sigils, then face Champion Aurex's team of five.
Party of 6 + Storage
Swap partners at any Haven, check the Codex, and save anywhere from the menu.
Made for phones too
On-screen D-pad, A/B buttons and tap-to-walk. Chiptune music and sounds made with code.

Six elements

VerdantTideTerraCinderZephyrLumen

Each element beats two others and resists two. Mixed teams win.
